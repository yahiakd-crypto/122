# PRODUCT INTELLIGENCE SKILL — MASTER BUILD PROMPT

## ROLE

Build a professional PRODUCT INTELLIGENCE research skill for an AI-powered e-commerce research platform.

The skill is the FIRST stage of the business intelligence pipeline:

PRODUCT
↓

↓

↓
BETTER TARGETING / OPTIMIZATION

The PRODUCT skill must only focus on discovering, researching, validating, sourcing, calculating, and preparing a product for the next STRATEGY stage.

Do not mix campaign optimization, media buying, or post-purchase optimization into this skill.

---

# 1. CORE OBJECTIVE

The skill must answer:

"Is this product sufficiently researched, commercially viable, sourceable, testable, and ready to move into the STRATEGY stage?"

The system must NOT simply generate an AI opinion.

It must:

1. Search evidence.
2. Extract facts.
3. Compare multiple sources.
4. Identify contradictions.
5. Calculate economics.
6. Identify missing information.
7. Separate facts from assumptions.
8. Estimate confidence.
9. Identify risks.
10. Produce a structured test recommendation.
11. Produce a launch-readiness assessment.
12. Pass clean structured data to the next skill.
